What it checks

The detector scores visible post text for formulaic filler, engagement bait, generic motivation, empty business jargon and pseudo insight. The score describes writing patterns. It does not prove whether AI was used.

Version 0.3.0

This release adds pseudo insight detection, hard negative guidance and a 500 post benchmark across English, Arabic, Turkish and mixed language text. It also adds benchmark validation tests and an optional live Jev benchmark runner.
