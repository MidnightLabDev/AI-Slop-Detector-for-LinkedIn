Direct Jev requests

When scanning is on, this extension sends text from visible LinkedIn posts directly to TypeSafe at api.typesafe.ai using your Jev API key. There is no shared backend or Cloudflare connection. Requests are billed to the TypeSafe account associated with your key.

The extension reads post text present in the page. It does not collect comments, messages, surrounding profile details or images. Personal details written inside a post body remain part of that text.

Key storage

By default, the key is kept in Chrome extension session storage. Chrome clears it when the browser restarts or the extension is reloaded, disabled or updated. If you select Remember on this device, it is saved in local extension storage until you remove it or uninstall the extension.

Both storage areas are restricted to trusted extension pages and its background worker. The key is not sent to LinkedIn, returned to the feed script or synced through Chrome sync. Local storage is not an encrypted password vault. Anyone with access to your browser profile or device may be able to recover a remembered key.

Use Remove in the popup to clear the saved key and turn off scanning. You can also revoke it from your TypeSafe account.

Other stored data

Preferences and daily request counters stay on your device. Classification results are cached in session storage for one hour, with up to 300 entries. The cache contains hashes and result data, not raw post text. Switching off stops queued work and attempts to cancel active requests. A request already accepted by TypeSafe can still be billed.

The popup shows a local request guard, not a provider billing limit. Clearing extension data resets it. Other uses of your API key are outside its scope.

What a score means

The five filters identify formulaic filler, engagement bait, generic motivation, empty business jargon and pseudo insight. Scores cannot establish whether a person used AI. Arabic, Turkish and mixed language results are experimental.

LinkedIn has restrictions on extensions that collect content or change its appearance. This prototype does not establish platform permission or store approval.
